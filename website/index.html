The approach

A photo starts the conversation.

Crop symptoms can look alike. Our approach connects what is visible in an image with relevant agricultural information and the context a farmer provides.

01

Share the crop image

Provide a clear photo of the affected plant and a question. Details such as crop stage and location can help explain the situation.

02

Analyse visible symptoms

A vision-language model examines crop characteristics and visible symptoms to suggest possible causes.

03

Connect to evidence

Retrieval-augmented generation brings relevant reference material into the response to support understandable guidance.

Behind CropGuard

Vision meets agricultural knowledge.

The hackathon project brings together crop-focused model adaptation, evidence retrieval and an interactive application.

Visual understanding

NVIDIA Nemotron model experiments and LoRA adaptation explore how multimodal AI can recognise crop-specific patterns.

Grounded responses

A RAG pipeline connects the image-analysis findings with agricultural reference material for more contextual answers.

Interactive experience

A Streamlit demo brings image upload, questions and model responses together, with GPU-backed inference on the hackathon cluster.

Research prototype: model selection, crop coverage and evaluation continue to evolve during the hackathon.

Responsible guidance

Useful insights begin with clear limits.

A photograph provides clues, but it cannot capture every condition affecting a crop. CropGuard is designed to support informed discussion and further investigation.

Make uncertainty visible

Similar symptoms may have different causes. Suggested diagnoses should be treated as possibilities to verify.

Consider local context

Crop stage, weather, soil conditions and regional practices can change which advice is appropriate.

Keep expert judgement involved

Confirm consequential treatment decisions with a qualified agricultural professional or local extension service.
